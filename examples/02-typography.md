# On Restraint

A single page on why less is often more — rendered through the same pipeline
that will produce your longer documents.

## The Case

Good design removes what doesn't earn its place. **Every element competes for
attention**, and *most attention budgets are small*. A page with fewer signals
lets the remaining ones mean more.

When the default is restraint, an accent — `inline code`, a blockquote, a rule
— lands with weight. When everything shouts, nothing does.

### On Mono

The `JetBrains Mono` face is reserved for code, the H1, labels, and the page
number. Used everywhere, it would read as terminal output. Used here, it marks
the signals of "system": things the reader is meant to parse precisely.

### On Sans

IBM Plex Sans carries the body. It is a reading face, not a display face. Its
job is to get out of the way.

## The Quote

> Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when
> there is nothing left to take away.

— Antoine de Saint-Exupéry, who flew mail planes.
